# Writing style: sound human, not AI

Applied across the whole site. When you (or I) write new copy, follow this.

## The tells to avoid

1. **Em dashes (—).** The single biggest signal of AI text. Use a period or a comma, or rewrite the sentence. A colon is fine when introducing a list. Keep em dashes to near-zero.
2. **"Not just X, it's Y" / "not only… but also."** This antithesis pattern is everywhere in AI copy. Just say the thing.
3. **Rule-of-three parallelism.** "Find the constraint, engineer the system, measure obsessively." One tidy triad is fine occasionally; stacking them reads like a machine.
4. **Hype and filler words.** Ban: leverage, unlock, seamless, elevate, robust, empower, tailored, cutting-edge, supercharge, game-changer, delve, realm, harness, "navigate the landscape," "in today's fast-paced world," "it's important to note," "at the end of the day."
5. **"Ta-da" turns.** "Here's the thing," "here's the truth," "but here's what nobody tells you." Cut them.
6. **Uniform sentence length.** AI writes 15–20 word sentences on repeat. Vary it. Short punches. Then a longer line that carries a bit more weight and rhythm.
7. **Hedging.** "Arguably," "quite," "really," "very," "generally." Take a stance instead.
8. **Overused connectors.** Moreover, furthermore, additionally. Usually deletable.

## What to do instead

- Write like you'd explain it to one smart client across a table.
- Contractions are good. So are short fragments, for emphasis.
- Concrete nouns and plain verbs beat abstractions.
- One clear call to action per page.
- Say the specific number or the specific thing. Specificity reads as human and expert.
