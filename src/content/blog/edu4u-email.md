## The client

Edu4u is a student-recruitment startup that connects international students with universities. Interest was never the problem. Prospective students found Edu4u, browsed, asked a question or two, and then went quiet. Most never came back.

## The challenge

A prospective student rarely decides on the first visit. Choosing where to study is a months-long decision, full of doubts and comparisons. Edu4u had no way to stay in that conversation, so all that early interest leaked away while the team kept chasing new traffic.

## What I did

I built the lifecycle email system that kept Edu4u in the room.

I mapped the real student journey, from first curiosity to application, and built automated flows for each stage: a welcome sequence that set expectations, nurture emails that answered the questions students actually agonise over, and timely nudges as deadlines approached.

I segmented by country and course interest so the message always felt personal, and tied everything back to enrolments so we knew which emails earned their place.

## The results

Email went from an afterthought to Edu4u's most profitable channel.

- Every **$1** spent on email returned **$8.40** in enrolment revenue.
- Annual revenue grew from **£22,000 to over £240,000** as more interest converted.
- Student sign-ups climbed from **14 a year to more than 80**.

> Email turned prospective students into enrolments while we slept. It changed the shape of the business.

The traffic was already arriving. We just stopped letting it walk away.
